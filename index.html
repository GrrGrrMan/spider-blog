Multimodal AI Hexapod

Low-Cost Autonomous Dangerous Environment Explorer

Project Aim

To design a low-cost, AI-powered hexapod (spider) robot to operate in dangerous environments, reducing human risk. Features live inverse kinematics, a custom FreeRTOS firmware, and a Multimodal AI loop (Groq/Llama 3.3) for voice-controlled movement.
