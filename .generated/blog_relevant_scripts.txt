date: "08/05/26"
title: "First Leg Prototype"
order: 2
---
The first set of components arrived. Used acrylic to cut out our first leg and attached the servos. It was hard to cut and size the acrylic board into the correct shape. 

Flashed and tried a simple moving command with the first leg and `pca9685` driver. The results proved that the acrylic connections were far too weak for load-bearing joints.
